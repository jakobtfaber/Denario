Investigate the effects of non-linear damping on the period of oscillation in simple harmonic oscillators. By simulating a range of mass and spring constant values, this study will focus solely on how varying degrees of non-linear damping coefficients influence the oscillation period compared to the traditional linear case. The research aims to provide insights into energy dissipation mechanisms in mechanical systems, with potential applications in engineering fields where non-linear behavior is significant, such as in the design of advanced materials and vibration control systems.
